FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission eligibility?
+

Students from Class 6 to Class 10 are eligible to enroll. We welcome students from CBSE, ICSE, and Karnataka State Board. A brief interaction session is conducted before confirmation to understand the student's current level.

What is the fee structure?
+

Fee varies by class and subjects. Please contact us directly at +91-9449522380 or via WhatsApp to get the current fee structure tailored to your child's class and board.

What are the class timings?
+

We have batches starting from evening (6:30–8:30 PM) batches, Monday to Friday and on saturday the timings will be from 4:00-7:00PM. Timings are allocated based on availability and student preference.

Do you provide study materials?
+

Yes! We provide comprehensive study materials, worksheets, practice tests, and previous year question papers. All materials are curated by our experienced faculty to align with the board curriculum.

How can I track my child's progress?
+

We conduct regular assessments and mock tests. Parents receive detailed performance reports and are invited for periodic parent-teacher meetings. You can also contact the faculty anytime for updates.
